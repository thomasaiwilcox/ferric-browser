Disposable WebAuthn RP fixture

This page uses only the browser's WebAuthn API and the fixture RP at this exact origin. It creates one temporary account, then independently verifies a sign-in.

For a phone passkey, keep this page on the desktop. Choose “Use a phone” in the browser's authenticator dialog, then scan its QR code with your phone's camera. The passkey may sync with your selected provider. After testing, remove it from that provider as well as deleting the disposable account below.
