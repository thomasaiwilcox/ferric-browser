## Supported scope to qualify

The concise proposed release boundary is also recorded in
[structural-release.md](structural-release.md); this task record tracks the
evidence needed before that draft can become an accepted milestone.

The milestone is limited to structural continuity in a durable normal profile:
typed structural commit publication, bounded commit listing/details, profile-
local deletion preview and collection, normal-profile context membership, and
restore into a fresh tab-capable window. Safe-GET replay requires the existing
exact navigation evidence; uncertain or unsafe locations remain placeholders.
Restore does not replay page bodies, form submissions, browser history, cookies,
or unknown live application state. Inactive restored pages remain lazy.

The capability does not imply page-archive capture/viewing, cookie continuity,
live-view retention, current-window replacement, arbitrary-site reconstruction,
or cross-profile tab adoption. Those remain under their own gates and evidence
records, including VB-024, VB-033, VB-044, VB-051, and VB-057. In particular,
current-window replacement must continue to fail closed until a qualified
live-retention path exists.
